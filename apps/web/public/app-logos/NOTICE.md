# App logo sources

Brand SVGs are vendored from Simple Icons (CC0-1.0), with brand-color fills added. Runtime rendering makes no third-party image requests; the assets are available offline.

- Simple Icons 13.21.0: Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Telegram, Slack, Discord, Notion, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Google Gemini, Ollama, OpenAI, Google.
- Simple Icons 9.21.0: Microsoft, Microsoft Outlook, Microsoft Excel.
- Upstream: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons
- Source format: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/VERSION/icons/SLUG.svg`.

Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Logos identify integrations and do not indicate endorsement or a connected account. SMTP, IMAP and HTTP are protocols, represented by the existing generic icons.
